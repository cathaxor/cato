# Cato v1.3.0 — The Royal Accessories & Living Companion Release 🐾🎩

Meet **Cato**, a native, ultra-lightweight, hardware-accelerated desktop cat companion engineered in C++20 using the Win32 API and OpenGL. Cato wanders your desktop, trails behind your cursor, perches on application window title bars, swats interactive physical toys, glides down with emergency parachutes when applications close, purrs with an in-memory procedural feline sound synthesizer, and wears custom pixel-art hats and glasses!

This release provides both an official Windows installer (`Cato-v1.3.0-Setup-x64.exe`) and a completely standalone portable archive package (`Cato-v1.3.0-Portable-x64.zip`) with **zero external dependencies** and zero admin rights required.

---

### 🌟 What's New in v1.3.0

1. **🎩 Custom Wearable Accessories (Hats & Glasses)**:
   - **🎩 Top Hat**: Classic gentleman black top hat with ruby crimson silk ribbon band and gold center buckle.
   - **🕶️ Sunglasses**: Sleek thug life pixel sunglasses with jet-black lenses, reflection glint, and nose bridge.
   - **🥳 Party Hat**: Festive striped cone hat with vibrant colors and an animated golden tassel/pom-pom!
   - **❄️ Winter Beanie**: Warm red knit winter beanie with snowflake cross stitches, cream cuff rim, and fluffy pom-pom.
   - **🎀 Bowtie**: Dapper crimson bowtie attached to Cato's collar with flaring wings and gold center clasp.
   - **👑 Royal Crown**: Imperial golden crown with 3 peaks, inlaid with Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald gems, featuring periodic royal shimmer sparkles!
   - **Equip Live Anytime**: Switch accessories via the **Accessories ▸** System Tray menu or the **Appearance** tab in Settings with live preview!

2. **🪂 Emergency Parachute Descent on Window Close / Minimize**:
   - When an application window Cato is perched on closes or minimizes, Cato immediately deploys its colorful parachute canopy, lets out an alert sound, and glides gracefully down to sit comfortably on your desktop floor!

3. **🖱️ Motion-Vector Cursor Wake Trailing ("Picone Picone Asbe")**:
   - Cato trails smoothly behind your cursor with organic Newtonian steering curves.
   - Dynamic Gait Switching: Relaxed walking pace when following closely, energetic sprinting with speed streaks when dashing across multi-monitor setups.
   - Automatic Sitting: Decelerates and curls into a cozy sit after 1.2s of mouse inactivity.

4. **🔊 Procedural Feline Audio Synthesizer**:
   - 8 authentic in-memory 16-bit 44.1kHz mono PCM WAV sounds (`Meow`, `Purr`, `Chirp`, `Swat`, `Jump`, `Hiss`, `Snore`, `Reminder`) with granular toggles, test buttons, and custom disk overrides.

5. **🧸 4 Interactive Desktop Toys**:
   - `Yarn Ball`, `Red Bouncy Ball`, `Feather Wand`, and `Laser Dot` with floor gravity, bounce physics, and momentum throwing.

6. **🎨 Modern Obsidian Glass Settings Interface**:
   - Windows 11 Acrylic / Mica backdrop, 11 categorized control tabs, and instant bidirectional synchronization with the System Tray.

### 🚀 Quick Start (How to Run)

1. **Option 1 (Recommended)**: Download and run **`Cato-v1.3.0-Setup-x64.exe`**.
2. **Option 2 (Portable)**: Download **`Cato-v1.3.0-Portable-x64.zip`**, extract to any folder, and double-click **`Cato.exe`**.
3. **Controls**:
   - **Right-Click Cato or Tray Icon**: Open the full context menu (Accessories, Personality Modes, Toys, Actions, Settings, Exit).
   - **Left-Click & Drag**: Pick up Cato by the scruff to place it anywhere on your desktop.
   - **Mouse Hover**: Pet Cato to make it purr!
   - **Double-Click**: Open the complete Glass Acrylic Settings GUI.
